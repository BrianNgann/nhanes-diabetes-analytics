# Executive Summary

Among 5,767 observed adults age 20+ with usable HbA1c, the unweighted sample was 60.1% Normal, 27.4% Prediabetes, and 12.5% Diabetes. After applying the NHANES phlebotomy weight and complex design, estimated population prevalence was 65.30% Normal (95% CI 62.11–68.34), 24.25% Prediabetes (21.91–26.77), and 10.45% Diabetes (9.21–11.84).

Observed burden was greatest among older adults and adults with obesity. The age 60+ / obese segment contained 1,040 participants and had 26.3% diabetes plus 39.1% prediabetes. This ranks it first by observed diabetes burden among age–BMI segments with at least 100 participants; no arbitrary composite priority score was used.

In the survey-adjusted complete-case model (n=4,783; 587 observed diabetes events), each 10 additional years of age was associated with 1.70 times the odds of diabetes (95% CI 1.62–1.80), and each 5 kg/m² higher BMI with 1.61 times the odds (1.51–1.72). Male sex, Mexican American and Non-Hispanic Asian comparisons, and lower education categories showed higher survey-adjusted odds relative to their reference groups. The Non-Hispanic Black comparison was clear in the unweighted model but crossed 1 after survey adjustment (OR 1.50, 0.93–2.43). Systolic BP, ever-smoking, and both sleep comparisons did not show clear survey-adjusted associations. These are associations and should not be interpreted causally.

## Decision-support implications

- Use age and observed demographic/socioeconomic disparities to target screening access and outreach, while avoiding biological or causal interpretations of social categories.
- Treat the large prediabetes group as a prevention opportunity.
- Use BMI as a potentially modifiable planning indicator; the model supports an independent association after adjustment, but not an intervention effect.
- Retain sample-size context on every group comparison and de-emphasize small groups such as underweight participants.
- Use the survey-weighted tables for population estimates and retain the unweighted tables only for sample description.
